📊 Vital Signs Reference & Quiz Lab

Adult & Geriatric Vital Parameter Reference Cards

CTE Year 1 Clinical Benchmarks
❤️

Heart Rate (Pulse)

HR
Standard Adult Range 60 - 100 bpm
Geriatric Baseline 60 - 90 bpm
Critical Red Flag Thresholds < 50 bpm (Bradycardia) or > 120 bpm (Tachycardia)
🩸

Blood Pressure

BP
Standard Adult Range Systolic < 120 / Diastolic < 80 mmHg
Geriatric Baseline 120 - 130 / 70 - 85 mmHg
Critical Red Flag Thresholds Systolic < 90 (Hypotension) or > 180 (Hypertensive Crisis)
🫁

Oxygen Saturation

SpO2
Standard Adult Range 95% - 100%
Geriatric & COPD Baseline 92% - 98% (COPD Target: 88% - 92%)
Critical Red Flag Thresholds < 90% (Hypoxemia / Severe Hypoxia Emergency)
😮‍💨

Respiratory Rate

RR
Standard Adult Range 12 - 20 breaths/min
Geriatric Baseline 15 - 22 breaths/min
Critical Red Flag Thresholds < 10 (Bradypnea) or > 28 (Respiratory Distress)
🌡️

Body Temperature

TEMP
Standard Oral Adult Range 97.6°F - 99.6°F (36.5°C - 37.5°C)
Geriatric Baseline 96.8°F - 98.6°F (Lower Baseline)
Critical Red Flag Thresholds > 100.4°F (Fever/Febrile) or < 95.0°F (Hypothermia)
🍬

Blood Glucose

CBG
Standard Fasting Range 70 - 99 mg/dL
Post-Prandial (2 hrs post meal) < 140 mg/dL
Critical Red Flag Thresholds < 70 mg/dL (Hypoglycemia) or > 250 mg/dL (Hyperglycemia)
