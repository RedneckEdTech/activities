Forehand vs. Backhand Technique

Forehand Welding (Standard Steel < 1/8") Torch points in the direction of travel (45° angle). Rod precedes the torch flame. Preheats joint ahead of the puddle.
Backhand Welding (Heavy Plate > 3/16") Torch points back toward completed weld puddle (45° angle). Rod remains between flame and puddle. Yields deeper penetration.
